uber: treat the meter-entry dialog as the end of the trip

Uber dispatches the next call before the driver arrives, so the driver finishes
the current trip, takes payment, then drives to the pickup he was already given.
He never returns to the Uber home screen, which is what trip completion was
waiting for. The trip stayed open and every following meter reading overwrote it.

Driver 592: three calls collapsed into trip 13604 - 8,200 then 8,400 then 8,800,
stored as one 46-minute trip of 8,800. 16,600 won of a 25,400 won stretch was
gone. Kakao does not show this because its "passenger boarded" tap already
triggers the stall-recovery path; Uber has no such button.

The meter dialog only ever appears after arrival, so it is finalized there. It
re-renders several times within a couple of seconds, so trip creation is held off
for 90 seconds while it is on screen, otherwise the repeats would open a phantom
trip on the spot. Mid-typing values stay guarded by the 3,000 floor and the
300-second correction window.
