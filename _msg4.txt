uber: stop rewriting a fare from the home screen

Driver 592 reported an 8,700 won trip turning into 36,800 by itself, which was
that day's Uber total rather than any single fare. The recovery path read the
line above the words "last trip" on the Uber home screen, so a running total
sitting in that position passed the 1,000-500,000 range check and overwrote the
trip silently.

Raising the Uber floor to 3,000 in v99 left recentFinalFare at zero more often,
which is the condition this path waits for, so a pre-existing bug started firing
regularly.

Removed rather than repaired: a wrong number written without the driver noticing
is worse than a zero he can see and fix, and card-approval notifications now fill
zero-fare trips from the actual charged amount.
